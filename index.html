About

About me

I’m Bima — a fresh graduate in Business Information Systems from Politeknik Negeri Malang. I focus on understanding how a business actually works day-to-day, then translating user needs into clear functional requirements and practical system solutions.

Over ~6 months of practical experience as an Odoo Functional Consultant Intern, I supported ERP implementation work across operational processes, including requirements discussions, configuration support, master data preparation, functional testing/UAT support, documentation, and user training. I’m comfortable communicating with different types of users and stakeholders — from operational teams to project peers.

My goal is simple: bridge the gap between business needs and technology so teams can make better decisions, adopt systems smoothly, and run clearer processes.
